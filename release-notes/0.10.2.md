# 0.10.2

TPS Controller now publishes native-notification schedules in the exact canonical order TishOS verifies and no longer reports a paired device ready when its schedule is unavailable.

- Equal-fire-time items use one raw UTF-8 byte comparator through candidate ordering, final signed-queue ordering, and validation. This removes locale-sensitive ordering of base64url `-` and `_` punctuation.
- Command-catalog success no longer masks schedule projection or publication failure. A client is ready only when both its exact command catalog and exact native-notification schedule verify.
- Failed schedule clients remain pending with `native-notification-schedule-unavailable` and retry automatically; successfully verified clients remain independently ready.
- Pairing UI notices distinguish saved authorization from completed publication.
- The wire schema, signatures, pairing credentials, reminder rules, settings, and note data are unchanged.

This is a backward-compatible reliability patch with no migration. Minimum supported Obsidian remains 1.12.0.

Validation passed the focused 46-check command-bridge suite, all 20 package-declared test files, TypeScript, and the required separate production-mode build, with the three preserved historical notification-comparison skips. The full suite deployed only byte-changed `main.js` and `manifest.json` to the isolated test runtime; the required standalone build then reported `[runtime-deploy] target=test ... unchanged`.
