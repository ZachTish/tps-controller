# TPS Controller 0.8.2

## Fixed

- Repeat-until-complete reminders now publish a bounded future occurrence series to paired TishOS devices instead of depending on Obsidian waking again after every repeat.
- An overdue five-minute rule therefore continues through Apple native notifications while Obsidian is suspended, until a later Controller publication removes it because the task is complete, won't-do, snoozed, disabled, filtered, or otherwise stopped by the configured rule.

## Safety and compatibility

- Reminder eligibility, provider choice, exact Controller rule semantics, signing, per-device pairing, 60-day horizon, 128-item signed schedule cap, and TishOS's separate 32-request cap are unchanged.
- Projection remains read-only and does not consume Controller delivery state or mutate the vault.
- Minimum Obsidian version remains 1.12.0. No settings, note-data, pairing, or wire-schema migration is required.

- The complete declared `npm test` chain passed, including TypeScript, command-surface, command-bridge, packaging, and production bundling gates. The mandatory separate final `npm run build` also passed and reported `[runtime-deploy] target=test ... unchanged` after the test deployment.
