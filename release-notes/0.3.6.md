# TPS Controller 0.3.6

TPS Controller `0.3.6` is a backward-compatible notification-opening performance and reliability patch. **View Notifications** now relies on one supported Obsidian workspace path and the view's normal initial refresh instead of repeating the full reminder scan.

## Changes

- Open a fresh notification leaf with public `ensureSideLeaf`, `revealLeaf`, `setActiveLeaf`, and `requestSaveLayout` APIs.
- Remove the obsolete `getRightLeaf` availability fallback, duplicate `setViewState`, direct `rightSplit` access, private parent-tab selection, redundant deferred load, and explicit second refresh.
- Keep concise start/done diagnostics without logging private workspace state.
- Reduce shipped `openNotificationModal` implementation by 37 lines, with no replacement abstraction or runtime state.

## Preserved behavior

- **View Notifications** remains available and still resets stale notification leaves.
- The notification view still opens in the right sidebar, is revealed and focused, and requests a saved layout.
- Empty results still render **All caught up!**; populated rows, sorting, manual refresh, metadata/vault refreshes, the 30-second refresh interval, row actions, reminder settings, and overdue evaluation remain unchanged.
- No command, setting, rule, action, API, stored field, or note-data format is removed.

## Before/after evidence

The executable lifecycle harness pins exact released `0.3.5` commit `a8c13dbda82caa95807d4fce912334b8feb9583e` and its `overdue-service.ts` SHA-256, extracts the production notification view type, then transpiles and runs the real `openNotificationModal` method body from that baseline and the candidate. Its deferred leaf loads on reveal and its simulated `onOpen()` performs the normal initial refresh:

- One 1,000-item open: `2` scans / `2,000` synthetic item visits to `1` scan / `1,000` visits.
- 320 opens over 20,000 synthetic reminder candidates: `12,800,000` item visits to `6,400,000`.
- Final rendered fingerprint, one fresh leaf, right-sidebar reveal, focus, layout-save request, and initial/reveal error propagation are unchanged.
- A baseline-only injected second-refresh failure now disappears with that redundant refresh surface.

These counts isolate command-triggered notification scanning. They are not vault file-read counts and do not claim that overall Controller or Obsidian latency is reduced by the same percentage. The harness reports timing for inspection, but sub-millisecond timing is deliberately not an acceptance gate.

## Compatibility and limitations

- Minimum supported Obsidian version remains `1.12.0`; all replacement APIs predate that minimum.
- No settings or data migration is required.
- The notification view continues to refresh when it opens, on its existing metadata/vault triggers, when manually requested, and every 30 seconds while open.
- The view now reflects the normal `onOpen` snapshot. A reminder changed after that scan but before command completion may wait for an applicable existing trigger, manual refresh, or the 30-second interval instead of being caught by the removed immediate second scan.
