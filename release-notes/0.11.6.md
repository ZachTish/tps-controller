# TPS Controller 0.11.6

Signed TishOS schedules now preserve finite **Repeat until scheduled time** cadences instead of stopping after the first projected occurrence. A rule that begins 15 minutes before its scheduled time and repeats every five minutes produces three deterministic one-shot occurrences at −15, −10, and −5 minutes. Follow-ups stop strictly before the scheduled time, matching Controller's foreground behavior, and `maxRepeats` still counts follow-ups after the initial occurrence.

This deliberately does not broaden any rule's source eligibility or migrate an unlimited post-end repeat rule. Calendar events must use the bounded cadence on the rule that owns their initial reminder; that avoids flooding the 128-item Controller lease and 64-request Apple lease with unlimited five-minute repeats.

The release adds production-shaped filtering and boundary regressions for exact cadence, catch-up, offset-zero initial reminders, repeat limits, and read-only projection. It changes no notification wire schema, pairing credential, note format, or existing rule automatically. Minimum Obsidian remains 1.12.0.
