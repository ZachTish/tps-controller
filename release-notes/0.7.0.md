# TPS Controller 0.7.0

## Notification service selection

- Replaces competing notification-delivery toggles with one persisted **Notification Service** selector.
- Ships an extensible provider registry with **TishOS** and **ntfy**.
- TishOS selection publishes the signed Controller-rule schedule and suppresses direct ntfy delivery.
- ntfy selection publishes an empty TishOS schedule before restarting the established desktop Controller/Messager route, preventing both services from being intentionally active at once.
- Keeps **Enable Reminders** as the sole on/off control and scopes hourly time-tracking reminder delivery to ntfy.
- Routes **Check Now** to an immediate signed schedule refresh when TishOS is selected.
- Shows provider-specific **Open TishOS** or **Open ntfy Settings** actions.

## Migration

- Fresh Controller installations default to TishOS.
- Existing settings without a provider preserve their established Messager/Notifier behavior by migrating to ntfy.
- The former `enableLocalReminderNoticesOnUserDevices` field is removed from persisted settings; reminder rules, Controller alert history, command-bridge pairing, and note data are unchanged.
