# 0.10.7

TPS Controller now resolves TishOS notification actions by durable reminder series after a repeating schedule advances.

- TishOS action protocol version 2 authenticates the exact fired occurrence plus its opaque series identity for Snooze and Complete.
- Controller keeps the fired occurrence in the signed request for audit and replay protection, then re-resolves the current writable reminder by series.
- Several future occurrences of one repeating reminder collapse to one source/reminder authority and deterministically select the earliest current occurrence. A series collision across different sources remains ambiguous and fails closed.
- Version-1 exact-occurrence requests remain accepted for compatibility. Stale, malformed, wrong-vault, revoked, replayed, external, terminal, and non-writable actions remain rejected.
- The ten-minute snooze property and completion mutation are still written by same-device Obsidian. Mac relay remains optional and read-only; normal vault sync propagates the mutation.

This release changes no pairing credential, signed notification-schedule schema, reminder rule, setting, or note format. Minimum supported Obsidian remains 1.12.0.

The focused 52-check bridge suite covers an already-fired repeat occurrence, multiple future occurrences in the same series, version-1 compatibility, replay protection, and fail-closed current-source resolution.
