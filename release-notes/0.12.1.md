# TPS Controller 0.12.1

TPS Controller now keeps reminders available on vault devices that cannot run TishOS without adding another synced notification-provider choice.

- With **Enable Reminders** on and **Notification Service → TishOS** selected, Windows, Linux, and Android evaluate the normal Controller reminder rules locally and display the existing clickable Obsidian notices.
- The fallback works in both User and Controller role, uses the existing vault-scoped device-local alert history, responds to normal lifecycle and policy restarts, and supports **Run Reminder Check Now** on the current device.
- The local route exits before TPS Messager/Notifier lookup and never sends through ntfy. macOS, iPhone, and iPad retain the existing TishOS-native schedule path, preventing parallel local/native consumption.
- Settings and the Notifications view report when the fallback is active and explain its limit: Obsidian must remain open. A local in-app notice cannot wake a closed or suspended desktop/mobile app and is not an operating-system notification-center alert.
- This change adds no setting or note migration, no new frontmatter field, no pairing or signed-schedule change, and no background-mobile capability. Minimum supported Obsidian remains 1.12.0.

## Validation and artifacts

- Focused platform/provider, reminder-engine, and command/settings regressions passed, including macOS/iOS/iPadOS versus Windows/Linux/Android, User and Controller roles, manual checks, lifecycle replacement, device-local state, and strict Messager bypass.
- The complete declared `npm test` suite, TypeScript, diff checks, mandatory separate production build, and isolated test-vault reload/UI checks passed.
- Release artifact SHA-256 values are recorded in the public GitHub release.

Known limitation: local Obsidian notices require the Obsidian process to remain open and active enough to run plugins; they cannot provide native background notification-center delivery.

Tested in the isolated test vault and ready for the user's exact BRAT pull. TishOS v0.1 was not inspected or directly deployed.
